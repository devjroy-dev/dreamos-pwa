P2 · the line beneathNEW · DOES TWO JOBS
Peers can find you by name while your storefront is public and this is on.

Job one: at default-on she is already listed, and is owed that fact before she has to ask for it. Job two: “while your storefront is public” is the third clause of the door’s predicate said plainly — if she has paused her storefront she is in no peer search regardless of this switch, because a vendor who un-published with the one control she was given has published nothing.

Why on by default at all: the search shows a peer only what her public storefront already shows a stranger — business name, handle, trade, city, every one of them on the public card’s own select. This lets her withdraw from a directory built from facts she already publishes; it grants no new exposure. 0140 defaulted the calendar check off because that opened a new fact to anyone. Same table, opposite default, one law — and 0142 says so out loud so the two columns never contradict in silence.
