The inbound-first outsider door

design item · no build in D2 or D3 unless the chair rules it

Today the outsider forward pushes a Marketing template at a vendor who has never written to TDW. That is the most throttled class Meta has, and 131049 is normal for it, not exceptional. The inbound-first door inverts the direction: the outsider opens the window, and TDW answers inside it with a free-form line and no template at all.

Founder sends a wa.me link or QR→ She taps it and messages TDW→ 24h window opens→ Free-form line carries the request→ She joins, and the lead materialises

What it buys

  • No template, no category. The Utility-versus-Marketing argument the founder raised at s1 stops applying, because a windowed reply is neither.
  • No per-user marketing cap. 131049 cannot fire on this path.
  • The request can be longer and can carry the brief in her own words.

What it costs

  • She must act first. A forward becomes a two-step ask, and the founder carries the first step by hand.
  • The window closes in 24 hours and cannot be reopened without a template.
  • It is a different product shape, not a re-filing of the existing one. Seat A said so at close and this sheet agrees.

The gate

A windowed free-form send has no template, so there is no template key to gate it on. The register key is therefore a flag.*, not a template.* — chair-ruled. Proposed name: flag.assist_inbound_door, seeded off, flipped on the Switchboard like every other plane.

What it does not change

  • The couple's phone still reaches nobody until that vendor joins.
  • createLead stays the sole lead writer.
  • No follow-up if she never taps. One ask, then silence.
Ruled by the chair, 2026-09-09. One standing TDW link — thedreamwedding.in/plan. One address the founder texts or prints as a QR and reuses forever. Per-request tokens are s3, not s2.
