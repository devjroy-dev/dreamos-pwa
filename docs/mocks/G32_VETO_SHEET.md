## 8 · THE EIGHTH TEMPLATE — one body, for Meta

**Category UTILITY. Ships DARK behind `CONTRACT_SEND_ENABLED`, unset in every environment, plus
`status: 'draft'` until Meta returns Approved. Two gates, and neither alone opens it.**

> **T1** — `{{1}} has sent you an agreement to read and sign. It covers {{2}}, and the deposit that
> holds your dates. Open {{3}} to read it — nothing is agreed until you tap I agree and enter the
> code we send you.`
>
> variables: `owner` · `functions` · `link`

**It opens and closes on prose by rule, not by luck.** Meta refuses a leading or trailing variable
— `wedding_credit` was re-authored on that wall on 2026-09-05 (F-40.91), and this body was written
to it rather than discovering it at submission.

**The Utility case, and its risk, stated rather than assumed.** This is a transactional notice about
a specific act by a specific person, naming what it concerns and how to decline by inaction. That
said, **F-19.07 and F-40.12 are this estate's own precedents for Meta reclassifying a Utility
template as MARKETING**, and this seat's prediction on `wedding_consent` was pessimistic and wrong.
The risk is recorded here, not discovered on submission.

**The OTP itself needs no eighth template.** Per R-G32.9 the registry gains `contract_sign_otp`
pointing at the **already-approved** `tdw_vendor_login_otp`, whose body is Meta's preset
`{{1}} is your verification code.` — a code and nothing else, naming no account. `tdw_contract_sign_otp`
is submitted in parallel and the key re-points on approval; both steps ride the build handover.
