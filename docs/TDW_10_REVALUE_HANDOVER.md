## 4 · CITATION-NEEDS-A-CELL, INSTRUMENTED

§2's pair set now covers every citation in the file that can be compared to a symbol: `bg-top`, `bg-mid`, `sheet`, `option-bg` join the fifteen. **19 pairs.**

Two mechanics the widening required, stated because they are very likely why the ground stops were never paired in the first place:

- **The comparison resolves the RGB TRIPLE, not the raw string.** A donor may be `rgba()` where the admin role must be opaque — a `<meta>`, an `<option>` and a gradient stop cannot carry alpha. String equality would have made these four cells impossible to write.
- **The one citation that cannot compare, COMPUTES.** `bg-bot`'s donor is a composite, not a symbol. The cell reproduces it from the exported symbols — `DARK.sheet` over `DARK.scrim` over `DARK.pageBg` → `rgb(18,15,14)` — so it cannot drift from its donors without reddening. A citation that computes outranks one that quotes.

One further cell added, unprompted: **the `--admin-bg` gradient's three stops must equal their own named tokens, in order.** The retint shipped a gradient carrying the same slip as the token beside it; nothing checked that the decomposition tokens and the thing actually rendered agreed.
