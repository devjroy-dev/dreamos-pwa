## 1 · F-41.94 — NO SWITCH SHARES A LINE WITH PROSE

**What the founder saw at 374:** *Answer couples on WhatsApp* broke to four lines and
`Anthropic · seeded` painted **over** the first of them, so the row read `Answerpic ·`.
Two strings, the same pixels.

right-hand `auto` column, and that row is itself `1fr auto` carrying the value text
**and** 168px of segments. The `auto` track sized to that max-content, the `1fr` title
was squeezed to min-content, and the two tracks together exceeded the box. A grid does
not clip in that state — it overlaps. `minWidth: 0` on the title could never help,
because the overflow was the other column's.

**This is F-41.67's class on the variant its cure never reached.** Seat E found it on
the tier row, gave the tier row a line of its own, and shot the frame. The flat row
kept the triptych because **in the mock it carries no value text beside the switch** —

**The cure is the same cure, applied where it was missed.** Every lane now renders as
a block spanning `1 / -1`, tiered or not; **nothing nests in the `auto` column at
all**. `flat` decides only whether a tier heading is drawn, never where a switch goes.
No pixel is named, no breakpoint added, no colour touched — `TDW_M_ROWFIX_PASS2`'s
discipline, where no cell names 374.

**Three surfaces are flat today** — the couple line, harvest, and the **bride app lane
F1b added**, which therefore arrives cured rather than arriving broken behind the
other two.

**The frame is in the packet** at `docs/mocks/COCKPIT/F2b-flat-row-374.html`, chair to
veto on this ZIP (R-40.101). It draws the defect as shipped, the cure across all three
