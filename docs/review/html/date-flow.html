Is a date free?

Today: 8 taps across Home, Rooms, Calendar and a day sheet that never says the word free. Proposed: 2 taps and a plain answer.

The steps

  1. 1Tap Check a date on Home.
  2. 2Pick the date. The answer says Free or Booked, and who asked for it.
  3. 3Open in calendar: booked, enquiry and blocked days drawn plainly; tap the month to jump.
