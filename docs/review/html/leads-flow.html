Enquiry to booked client

From Home: read the new enquiry, reply, then book it with a package. Today this takes 7 taps and three stacked sheets; proposed, 4 taps, and booking also makes the invoice and the calendar days.

The steps

  1. 1Home shows the enquiry and its last message. Tap it.
  2. 2The enquiry: the free date and the conversation come first. Tap Reply, or Book.
  3. 3One sheet: pick the package; fee, dates and advance are filled in. Tap Book and make invoice.
  4. 4The client page: money, functions with crew, the invoice.
