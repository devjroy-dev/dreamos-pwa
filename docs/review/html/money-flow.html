Send an invoice, see who owes

Today: 2 taps to the Invoices room and 8 to send one, with the client typed by hand. Proposed: Money is a tab; send from the client in 3 taps.

The steps

  1. 1Money: what is owed, by whom, by due date. Tap a client.
  2. 2The client: paid and due in one line. Tap Send reminder.
  3. 3The invoice and a ready message. Tap Send on WhatsApp.
