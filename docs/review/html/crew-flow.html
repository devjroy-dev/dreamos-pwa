Today's and this week's events, with crew

Today: Home lists the events with no crew; the crew is 4 taps away per event, inside the Calendar. Proposed: the crew is on the Home cards, the week is one tap, the event page one more.

The steps

  1. 1Home: each event shows its crew, confirmed or not yet replied. Tap This week.
  2. 2This week: every function by day, with its crew or a clear No crew yet. Tap an event.
  3. 3The event: where, when, the client, the crew, Message crew.
