## 6 · ⑤ F-13.11 — TWO SITES, AND THE THIRD NAMED

Its own commit, first in the sequence.

**Half one:** the lazy `useState` initializer read `navigator` during the first
render — one answer on the server, another in the browser. `typeof
navigator==='undefined'` was a guard against a CRASH, not against the mismatch:
it made the server render succeed with the wrong answer, which is the harder
version to see. Now `false` on both first renders, by construction, real answer
after mount.

**Half two:** the activity dot compared `Date.now()` in render at two sites. The
two clocks are never the same instant, so a row near the ten-minute boundary drew
the dot on one render and not the other — intermittent, which is worse to chase.
`nowTs` is null until mount and **refreshes on the screen's existing 10s tick**;
R-D3.5's one-timer ruling holds, and §8.12 asserts no second `setInterval`
appeared.

**The third `Date.now()` is classified and left** (chair correction c-33.7). It is
inside `CircleCompose`'s `onSent` callback: a thumb press, in the browser, long
after hydration, with no server render to disagree with. §8.8–§8.11 assert the
count is three and **name every survivor's context**, so a bare "zero Date.now()"
cell can never force a cure on a non-defect to satisfy its own wording.
